# Project Affiliation is now part of OSL Alliance

OSL has combined its former **Project Affiliation** and **Project Incubation** programs into the **[OSL Alliance](/projects/alliance/)**. This simplifies administration and gives projects one place to find the same core community benefits and opportunities, while introducing the Alliance Maintainers Council for reciprocal support of OSL activities.

For program details and new applications, visit the [OSL Alliance page](/projects/alliance/) or the [Alliance requests repository](https://github.com/OpenScienceLabs/alliance-requests). Existing projects appear together in the [Alliance directory](/projects/list/).
