# Learning with FreeCampus

At Open Science Labs (OSL), we believe that access to knowledge and practical
skills helps people participate more fully in open science and open-source
communities. Education is an important part of that mission, and we are pleased
to collaborate with **[FreeCampus](https://freecampus.org/)**, an initiative
dedicated to making high-quality learning materials freely accessible to
everyone.

Rather than developing a separate course and training program on the OSL
website, we are directing people interested in learning and educational
collaboration to FreeCampus. This allows us to focus on connecting people and
supporting open-source projects while helping learners and educators discover
a community whose work centers on open education.

## Discover FreeCampus

FreeCampus brings together learners, educators, and contributors to create
open, thoughtfully structured courses and learning resources. Its approach
emphasizes strong foundations, clear explanations, hands-on practice, and
materials that the community can review and improve over time.

You can explore its growing collection of subjects, including **Python and
mathematics**, learn at your own pace, and find ways to help make educational
resources better and more accessible.

**[Explore FreeCampus courses and subjects →](https://freecampus.org/subjects/)**

## Have an idea for a course or training initiative?

Whether you want to propose a new course, contribute teaching materials,
collaborate on a workshop or training initiative, or share your expertise as
an educator, **please contact the FreeCampus community directly**. FreeCampus
coordinates course proposals, educational content, and related learning
initiatives through its own channels.

- **[Visit FreeCampus](https://freecampus.org/)** to discover its mission and
  learning resources.
- **[Explore the Instructor Network](https://freecampus.org/instructors/)**
  to learn how educators and practitioners can contribute.
- **[Read the Community Guide](https://freecampus.org/community-guide/)**
  for guidance on course ideas, contributions, and getting in touch.

We encourage everyone who believes in open, collaborative education to explore
FreeCampus, share its resources, and consider contributing. Together, we can
help more people access the knowledge and skills they need to learn, create,
and contribute to the world around them.
