# OSL Alliance

Open Science Labs (OSL) has combined its former **Project Affiliation** and
**Project Incubation** programs into one program: the **OSL Alliance**. The two
programs offered many of the same community benefits, but maintaining separate
applications, policies, and project categories created unnecessary work for OSL
and made it harder for maintainers and contributors to understand what support
was available. One program and one project directory make that relationship
clearer and easier to maintain.

**The benefits remain largely the same.** Alliance projects can connect with
OSL's network of maintainers and contributors, receive community visibility,
share knowledge and infrastructure, and seek opportunities for collaboration and
participation in programs such as Google Summer of Code (GSoC). Opportunities
depend on availability and program-specific requirements; membership does not
guarantee funding, mentors, contributors, or GSoC slots. Incubation-style
guidance and project development support may still be arranged where OSL has
capacity.

## A reciprocal relationship

The main addition is the **Alliance Maintainers Council**. Member projects will
be invited to designate a maintainer representative who can help OSL coordinate
priorities and sustain the organization. Alongside supporting their own
projects, Alliance maintainers are expected to contribute where they can:
reviewing applications and project proposals, mentoring contributors, answering
community questions, improving websites, bots or shared tools, supporting events
and programs, sharing knowledge, outreach, or helping identify partnerships and
funding opportunities.

Projects can contribute according to their expertise and capacity; no project is
expected to do all of these activities. OSL will work with maintainers to
establish a lightweight way to coordinate and recognize contributions, with
clear communication and reasonable time to adapt as processes improve. The
Council supports collaboration and provides input to OSL leadership; it does not
automatically confer a seat on OSL's legal governing board.

## What stays the same

Projects continue to own their roadmaps, technical decisions, releases,
maintenance, and governance. Joining the Alliance does not transfer project
ownership or ongoing maintenance responsibility to OSL. Projects must remain
open source, align with OSL's mission, maintain a Code of Conduct, and have a
reachable maintainer and public contribution or contact path.

## Join the OSL Alliance

To request membership, open an issue in the
[OSL Alliance requests repository](https://github.com/OpenScienceLabs/alliance-requests).
Include the project's repository, license, Code of Conduct, maintainer contact,
description, and the kind of collaboration it seeks.

Existing affiliated and incubated projects will be brought together in the
[Alliance project directory](/projects/list/). The transition does not
automatically remove existing opportunities or alter project governance. OSL
will communicate any practical onboarding or Council arrangements with
maintainers.
